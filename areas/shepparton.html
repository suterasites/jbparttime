Shepparton & the Goulburn Valley

Into the food bowl, and back out again.

Shepparton is the centre of the Goulburn Valley, one of Victoria’s biggest food-growing regions. Runs leave from our Hillside base on Melbourne’s north-western edge and head up the highway overnight, so bread and fresh stock arrive before the morning trade.

Fruit, produce and chilled food from the valley travel in refrigerated bodies, and freight and courier jobs run to Mooroopna, Kyabram, Tatura and the towns between.
