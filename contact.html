Questions

Before you get in touch.

Anything not covered here, call us on 0468 772 402.

How do I get a quote?

Fill in the form on this page with your pickup and drop-off points, how often you need it run and whether it needs refrigeration, or call us on 0468 772 402.

What areas do you cover?

All of Victoria. We run out of three bases, Hillside in Melbourne’s north west, Colac in the west and Albury on the NSW border, covering Mornington, Geelong, Warrnambool, Casterton, Shepparton and everywhere in between.

Do you take regular contracts or one-off jobs?

Both. Most of our work is regular scheduled runs for bakeries and food businesses, and we also take one-off freight, cold and courier jobs.

When can I reach you?

The office is open Monday to Saturday, 9am to 5pm. The deliveries themselves run overnight.
