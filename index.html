How a bakery run works, start to finish.

Stage What happens Status
01 Evening Loaded at the bakery Bread, rolls and pastry racked into the truck as the ovens finish. Loading
02 Late night Doors shut, temperature set Chilled lines go into refrigerated bodies and stay cold the whole way. Sealed
03 Overnight On the road Runs leave Hillside and Colac and fan out across the state while the roads are clear. En route
04 Before open Delivered to the door Dropped at the store, cafe or dock where you need it, ready for the morning. Delivered

Questions

Before you send a quote.

Anything not covered here, call Jesse on 0468 772 402.

Do you run overnight bread delivery for bakeries?

Yes. Overnight bakery delivery is the core of what we do. We load after the bake and run through the night, so bread, rolls and pastry arrive ahead of the morning trade.

Can you carry chilled goods?

Yes. 12 of our 25 vehicles are refrigerated, so chilled lines such as dairy, meat and produce stay cold from pickup to drop-off.

What areas do you cover?

All of Victoria. We run out of two bases, Hillside in Melbourne's north west and Colac in the state's west, covering Mornington, Geelong, Warrnambool, Casterton, Shepparton and everywhere in between.

Are the goods insured in transit?

Yes. We carry goods in transit and marine cargo insurance, along with WorkCover.

Do you take on regular contracts or one-off jobs?

Both. Most of our work is regular scheduled runs for bakeries and food businesses, and we also take one-off freight and courier jobs.
