Service questions

Before you book.

Anything not covered here, call us on 0468 772 402.

Which service do I need?

If it is bread, rolls or pastry going out overnight, that is a bakery run. If it has to stay chilled, it goes in one of our 12 refrigerated bodies. Larger or regular loads are freight, and parcels and smaller consignments are courier. Not sure? Call us on 0468 772 402 and we will work it out with you.

Do you only do contract runs?

No. Scheduled contract runs are the bulk of the work, and the same trucks take on one-off freight and courier jobs in between.

Are loads insured?

Yes. Every load is covered by goods in transit and marine cargo insurance, and our drivers are covered by WorkCover.

Where do you deliver?

All of Victoria, from three bases: Hillside on Melbourne’s north-western edge, Colac in the west and Albury on the NSW border.
