About JB Parttime

A transport company built on the night shift.

JB Parttime Pty Ltd has been moving food and freight around Victoria since 2008. The heart of the work is the overnight bakery run: loading bread as the ovens finish and getting it to the shelf before the doors open. Everything else we do is built on the habits that job demands.

The fleet now runs to 25 vehicles, 12 of them refrigerated, working out of two bases: Hillside, in Melbourne’s north west, and Colac, in the west. Between them they cover the whole state, from the Mornington Peninsula to Casterton and north to Shepparton.

It is still a business where quotes, new runs and problems are handled directly, not passed through a call centre.
