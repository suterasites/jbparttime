Coverage questions

Where we run to.

Anything not covered here, call us on 0468 772 402.

What areas do you cover?

All of Victoria. We run from two bases, Hillside on Melbourne’s north-western edge and Colac in the west, across seven regions from Mildura to Portland, and everywhere in between.

Which base will my run come from?

Usually the closer one. Hillside covers Melbourne, the Peninsula, Geelong, central Victoria and the north. Colac covers the Western District, the Surf Coast, the Great Ocean Road and the far south-west.

My town isn’t listed. Can you still deliver?

Almost certainly. The towns here are the ones we name, not the only ones we run to. Call us on 0468 772 402 and ask.
