Freight delivery

Your freight, on roads we run every night.

Freight is only as good as the carrier moving it. A load that turns up late, damaged or not at all costs you a customer, not just a delivery. JB Parttime has been moving goods for Victorian businesses since 2008, from single pickups to regular routes that run every week.

Our trucks already cover the state every night for bakery and refrigerated work, so we know the highways, the regional towns and the back docks. Your freight goes with a crew that does this for a living, not a driver finding the address for the first time.

Runs leave from our bases in Hillside, in Melbourne’s north west, and Colac, in the west, so freight between Melbourne and Geelong, Warrnambool, Shepparton or the coast starts close to the work.

Freight questions

Before you book a freight job.

Anything not covered here, call us on 0468 772 402.

What kind of freight do you carry?

General freight for businesses: cartons, pallets, stock, supplies and retail orders. If you are not sure whether your load fits, call us on 0468 772 402 and ask.

Do you deliver freight to regional Victoria?

Yes. We run freight right across Victoria from three bases, Hillside in Melbourne’s north west, Colac in the west and Albury on the NSW border, covering Mornington, Geelong, Warrnambool, Casterton, Shepparton and everywhere in between.

Do you do regular freight runs or one-off loads?

Both. Regular routes on set days are most of the work, and one-off loads are taken on too.

Is my freight insured?

Yes. Every load is covered by goods in transit and marine cargo insurance, and our drivers are covered by WorkCover.

Can you carry chilled or food freight?

Yes. 12 of our 25 vehicles carry refrigerated bodies, so food and temperature-sensitive freight can stay cold the whole way.

How is freight delivery priced?

Every job is quoted on its own: the pickup point, the number of drops, the distance, how often it runs and the size of the load. Send the quote form or call us on 0468 772 402.
