General courier

Small consignments, same standards.

A parcel can matter as much as a full truck. The part a job is waiting on, the order a customer paid for, the samples that have to arrive today. JB Parttime picks up and delivers parcels and smaller consignments across Victoria, with the same care we put into an overnight bakery contract.

Courier work is handled by the same crew and the same fleet that have been running Victorian routes since 2008. Every consignment is insured, and if it needs to stay cold, it can travel in one of our refrigerated vehicles.

With bases in Hillside, in Melbourne’s north west, and Colac, in the west, we courier across metro Melbourne and out to regional towns that a lot of city couriers won’t touch.

Courier questions

Before you book a courier.

Anything not covered here, call us on 0468 772 402.

What can you courier?

Parcels, satchels, documents, cartons, parts and other smaller consignments. Bigger loads go as freight. If you are not sure which, call us on 0468 772 402.

Can you set up regular courier pickups?

Yes. We can collect on set days for businesses that send things regularly, and take one-off jobs as well.

Do you courier to regional Victoria?

Yes. We courier right across Victoria from three bases, Hillside in Melbourne’s north west, Colac in the west and Albury on the NSW border, covering Mornington, Geelong, Warrnambool, Casterton, Shepparton and everywhere in between.

Are courier consignments insured?

Yes. Every consignment is covered by goods in transit and marine cargo insurance, and our drivers are covered by WorkCover.

Can you courier chilled items?

Yes. 12 of our 25 vehicles are refrigerated, so chilled samples, food orders and temperature-sensitive parcels can stay cold the whole way.

How are courier jobs priced?

Every job is quoted on its own: the pickup point, the number of drops, the distance, the size of the consignment and how often it runs. Send the quote form or call us on 0468 772 402.
